Portal Rewards redesign prototype
=================================

portal-rewards.html   The whole prototype in one file. Double-click to open it in Chrome or Edge.
                      It needs an internet connection: three.js (3D), the QR library and the
                      Google fonts load from public CDNs.

Live version (private until you share it):
https://claude.ai/artifact/SyvRXSS6Rw7B8Di3hexNun

source/               The parts the file is assembled from, in this order:
                      part1-style.html, part2-markup.html, part3a-coin.js, part3a-wormhole.js, part3b-page.js
                      part3a-gate.js is the earlier "Gate" portal (metal ring, chevrons). Use it in place of
                      part3a-wormhole.js to get that version back.
