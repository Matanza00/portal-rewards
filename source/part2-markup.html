Why a coin

The channel they can't switch off.

Three reasons brands put a coin inside the package instead of buying another impression.

Every purchase of your product generates first-party data right now. You just don't receive it. A coin inside the package reroutes that moment: the customer scans, enters your portal and identifies themselves to you, verified by the product in their hand, not a guess from a budtender's system.

Your product already reaches the customer. Now your brand does too.

No Google. No Meta. No billboards near a school. But the inside of your own bag? Unrestricted, unbannable, and already in your customer's hands at the exact moment they like you most. The coin turns that placement into currency they can actually spend with you.
